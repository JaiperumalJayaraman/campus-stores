PM CASE STUDY / 03

Three lenses. One product decision.

A full APM case study separates what has been prototyped from hypotheses requiring user interviews and production validation.

◉

UX · Desirability

Creators need to trust translations, especially terminology. Test whether segment-level correction is easier than redoing an entire dub.

Research task5 creator usability interviews; measure errors caught and task success.
⌘

Tech · Feasibility

Build a deterministic transcript review pipeline first. Later explore alignment with timecodes and regenerated speech for edited lines.

Engineering trade-offManual edits are low-cost; localized TTS has voice, timing and fidelity risks.
↗

Business · Viability

Better creator confidence could increase multilingual publishing and worldwide engagement. This remains a hypothesis.

Success metricReviewed dubs published per active dubbing creator per month.

What would I ship first?

MVP: transcript comparison, glossary warnings, local correction, review approval, export. Not in MVP: real YouTube publishing, voice cloning, automated translation, production TTS.
