# UX research plan · EntranceIQ

**Research status: Not conducted.** This prototype does not contain real campus gate information.

## Recruiting

Recruit 5–8 first-time visitors to a large campus or hospital, plus 2 staff/security experts to validate access policy assumptions. If piloting at IIT Madras, obtain permission and verify gate policies **before** showing any real-route instructions.

## Discovery interview prompts

1. When did navigation lead you near a destination but not its usable entrance?
2. What did you do next? Did signage, a phone call, or staff help?
3. Which information would have prevented the mistake?
4. Are your access needs different when walking, driving, or delivering?
5. Would gate-specific instructions feel useful or overwhelming?

## Prototype task script

- T1: Plan a visitor-driving route to the Learning centre from the West. Expected: any displayed gate must allow both visitor and driving access.
- T2: Plan a delivery-driving route to Innovation hall from the East. Expected: only the service gate is eligible.
- T3: Change delivery driving to delivery walking. Expected: clear no-route state, not an unsafe recommendation.
- T4: Compare alternatives and pick one; state why it is allowed.
- T5: Report an incorrect gate restriction and export feedback CSV.

Record: task success rate, seconds-to-correct-gate, misclicks, SEQ score (1–7), understanding of access restrictions, errors, and participant comments.
